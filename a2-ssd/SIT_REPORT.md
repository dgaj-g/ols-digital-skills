# Sit report — Normalisation (v14), walked as a pupil at 1280×800, 10 Nov 2026

**What a pupil sees**
1. Home: three topic tiles, SQL, Normalisation and Coursework guide, each with "Not started" until she begins.
2. Normalisation: the papers 2015–2026 in order, each with its parts and her marks. "Back to the topics" returns home.
3. A paper: its parts (a), (b), (c), each marked on its own.
4. A part, first visit: the exam document on the left; "Learn it first" (a short lesson on a nursery rota) or "Do it now".
5. Lesson: numbered steps (What repeats → First table → Group tables → Nested group → Quick checks → Your turn). The left panel has a "Lesson example" tab for the rota during teaching, and switches to the exam document for the quick checks.
6. Your turn: the exam document stays on the left with the fields tray under it (repeating groups in braces). On the right, "Add a table", a Left out box, and "Check my answer". The button stays grey until every field is placed, and shows why ("20 fields are still in the list.").
7. After marking: the examiner's marks, one line per point, each lost point with its hint. Then "Another question", a made-up practice question on its own document.
8. Theory parts (pick, click, steps, braces, why-not, split a field) use the same screen with the same Check button rule.

**Where she would stall**
- **Your turn on a big paper (2026 Q8, 20 fields).** An empty right-hand side is the moment to hesitate. The line beside "Add a table" says what to do; Help adds "drag each field into a table".
- **Help open at 1280 px.** The work narrows to about 400 px beside the Help column. Everything still fits and nothing is covered; she closes Help to get the room back.
- **2024 and 2025 documents.** The wide tables wrap their columns at 12 px. They are readable, but she may scroll the document to find a row.
- **Steps (2021 a, 2024 b).** A drop on a full stage swaps the old statement back to the bank. A pupil expecting "add" may be surprised once, but nothing is lost.

**Checked on the walk:** no page errors; no dialog boxes; Check my answer is the only greyed control and shows its reason; the document never leaves the left.
