# SIT REPORT — J2 Lesson 6, The Headliner (26 October 2026)

How it was sat: the served hour walked end to end at 1280×800, 1440×900 and 1024×768, once answering right and once taking every wrong path first (`j2l6-walk.js` and `--fails`), plus the platform walk around it (`j2l6-platform.js`: sign-in tile, Do now, the hour, five questions, how-did-it-go, free time). Screens read by eye at the smallest size.

## What a pupil sees
1. Do now: five questions from earlier lessons.
2. The job: what the hour is for, then Aoife from the Lumen festival asks for help. Her planner's laptop has stopped working.
3. A three-screen tour of the sheet (boxes, the Sum bar, the Plan and Shop tabs).
4. Part 1 · The grid. She watches =B3*C3, types it into D3, then makes two changes (tickets and price) and sees D3 follow without retyping.
5. Part 2 · The copy. She fills D3 down by dragging or with Fill down. She makes the totals in D6 and D11 and the profit in D12, guessing each answer first. The profit comes out at −1,700.
6. Part 3 · The decision. IF in D13 shows Book her or Not yet. She tests it with a £9,000 fee, then puts £18,000 back.
7. Part 4 · The call. Ivy Rain's manager asks three what-if questions, 90 seconds each. When a clock runs out, the same question returns with no clock and hints on.
8. Part 5 · Pin a box. She adds VAT on the Shop tab. Filling down goes wrong on purpose (B1 moves to B2), then $B$1 pins it.
9. The festival poster, badges, the recap, five last questions, how it went, then free time with three challenges.

Modelled time: 53 screens. About 11.5 minutes for the straight path and 11.8 minutes taking every wrong path. That leaves the class hour room for reading at a Year 9 pace.

| Place | Stall | What catches her |
|---|---|---|
| Part 1, D3 | Types 30000 instead of a sum | The judge says the number is right but typed, and asks for a sum starting with = |
| Part 2, fill | Cannot drag the small square | The Fill down button does the same job. The hint gives the mouse steps |
| Part 2, D12 | Surprised by a minus number | She guessed first, and the line says the money out is bigger than the money in |
| Part 3, IF | Brackets and commas | Three hints. The last one shows the exact sum to type |
| Part 4, clock | Runs out of time | The same question comes back with no clock and hints on. Nothing is lost |
| Part 5, $ | Cannot find the $ key | Hint: hold Shift and press 4 |
| Part 5, fill | Prices go wrong after fill down | This is meant to happen. The next screen shows the pin |

No dead end was found. Every screen has a way on, and a wrong answer always gets a next step. At 1024×768 the row of parts wraps onto two lines but stays readable. No new stall was found, so nothing was left to fix.
