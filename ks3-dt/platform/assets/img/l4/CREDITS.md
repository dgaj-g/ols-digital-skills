  into the operators' log book ("First actual case of bug being found").
  U.S. Navy photo, courtesy of the Naval Surface Warfare Center, Dahlgren, VA.
  Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:First_Computer_Bug,_1947.jpg
