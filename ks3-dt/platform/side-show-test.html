What to look for

Every picture here moves — Fred and Margo both blink, and the offended pair use a slow one. If a picture is frozen, that is the fault. The five expressions are meant to be told apart at 132 pixels without reading a caption: idle, typing (eyes down on the work), delighted (eyes become arcs), offended (one eyebrow up), and for Fred devastated (the aerial gives up). Margo has no devastated state on purpose: her Lesson 3 exit is the rave.
