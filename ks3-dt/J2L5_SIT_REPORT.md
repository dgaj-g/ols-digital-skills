## What a pupil sees, in order
1. **Recap.** The usual recap questions from earlier lessons.
2. **Do now.** The film opens the hour: the bang, and the lamb is lost. She names the lamb.
3. **1 The tracks.** BEEP flies the same flight again and again. She meets the for line, `for i in range(20):`, and sees that one line says how many times.
4. **2 The fields.** Three fields (square, sheep pen, three-cornered field). She counts the walls and types the number in the for line. A wrong count leaves a wall dark; the fault line under the field tells her why. Three wrong goes in the chapter and the screen goes black, rewinds, and her own lines are back in the box.
5. **3 The spiral.** The letter i grows the flight each time round. Held screens show "When i was 0 … When i was 9 …" line by line.
6. **4 The way home.** `while True` runs until she presses Stop it or the battery runs out. She predicts first, then runs.
7. **Exit check**, then **How it felt.** Finish here goes straight to the end; **Free flight** (three examples or her own loop) is the stretch for anyone with time left.

## Where she could stall, and what happens
- **A wrong count in The fields.** Not a dead end: the fault line names the wall that stayed dark, her lines stay in the box, and after the black screen she starts the field again with her own program.
- **Running out of the hour.** After minute 48 every chapter end offers Carry on or Home. A reopen at Home comes back to Home, and her place is saved.
- **A note that stayed up (found and fixed).** Notes now leave after six story seconds; chapter 3 clears its note before a fault line and before the held screen. The words gate had read 53 words on 3.3 held and 47 on 3.2 because of it.
- **The black screen (found and fixed).** Chapters 2 and 3 now clear the old fault line and the plan request when the screen goes black, so the rewind starts clean.
- **Stop it while still predicting.** The Stop it button comes up while the screen is still the prediction screen. Pupils see it; the walker now waits for it there too.
